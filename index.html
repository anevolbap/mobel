Keys and touch

Keyboard

  • Arrows nudge 5 cm (Shift = 1 cm) · R rotate 90°
  • X / Del delete · D / C duplicate
  • Ctrl/⌘+Z undo · Ctrl/⌘+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redo
  • Ctrl/⌘+A select all · Ctrl/⌘+S save a layout.json
  • M measure tape on and off
  • Esc clears the tape, then deselects
  • Shift+click adds to the selection · Shift+drag draws a selection box
  • Alt while dragging or measuring: no snap
  • Rearrange preview: ← / → step · Enter apply · Esc cancel
  • Walk: W A S D or arrows walk · Shift runs · Esc frees the mouse
  • ? opens this list · Esc closes it

Touch

  • Tap to select
  • Drag an object to move it, or empty space to pan
  • Pinch with two fingers to zoom, move both to pan
  • 3D: drag to turn around, pinch to zoom
